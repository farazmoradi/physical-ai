# Kite ML

- **Site:** https://kiteml.com/ (docs: https://kiteml.com/docs)
- **HQ:** San Francisco
- **Founders:** Raul Romero, Luigi D'Introno (co-founders per LinkedIn)
- **X:** [@trainrobotsfast](https://x.com/trainrobotsfast)
- **Category:** Robot learning tooling / developer platform

## What they do in physical AI

Positioned as **"The IDE for autonomous robots."** A platform for robotics teams to go from data to deployed policy:

- **Dataset augmentation** – augment LeRobot datasets.
- **Training** – train robot policies (RL in simulation; imitation policies such as ACT and π0) with on-demand cloud GPUs.
- **Simulation & digital twins** – build MuJoCo digital twins.
- **Evaluation** – evaluate policies in sim; "Kite ML Evaluations" beta, benchmarking models on a robot eval benchmark.
- **Deployment.**

## Supported robots / models

- Robots: Unitree G1 (humanoid), Unitree Go2 (quadruped), Boston Dynamics Spot, SO-100/SO-101 arm templates, custom URDFs.
- Model registry: Physical Intelligence π0 and π0-FAST; ACT and Pi policies trained via API on teleoperated arms.

## Developer surface

- CLI on PyPI: [`kiteml-cli`](https://pypi.org/project/kiteml-cli/)
- MCP server, so AI coding agents can drive training/sim/augmentation from the terminal.

## Take

Picks-and-shovels play: a "Vercel/Weights & Biases for robot policies" layered on the open-source robot learning stack (LeRobot, MuJoCo, π0). Bets on the explosion of teams fine-tuning generalist robot foundation models.
