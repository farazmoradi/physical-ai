# Veeda AI

- **Site:** https://www.veeda.ai/
- **HQ:** Toronto
- **Incorporated:** June 2026 (as Veeda Innovation)
- **Team:** Sanja Fidler (CEO, ex-head of NVIDIA's Toronto AI lab), Zan Gojcic (CTO), Huan Ling (Chief Scientist). All ex-NVIDIA.
- **Funding:** $90M+ seed (announced Aug 2026). Radical Ventures, Khosla Ventures. Dealroom lists a $500M–1B valuation.
- **Category:** World models for physical AI

## What they do in physical AI

Building **world models**: foundation models that **simulate physical reality**, so robots and other embodied agents can train and be evaluated in high-fidelity virtual environments instead of slow, costly, risky real-world trial and error.

The founders come from NVIDIA's Toronto lab, which did work on neural reconstruction / generative 3D and simulation (Omniverse/Cosmos-adjacent research), so expect generative, video- or 3D-native simulators rather than hand-built physics engines.

## Take

The best-funded and most "frontier-lab" of this group. Competes in the world-model race (NVIDIA Cosmos, Google DeepMind Genie, World Labs, Runway, etc.), aimed specifically at robotics training.

## Caveats

- Website reportedly says stealth mode and doesn't name the team; press coverage does.
- Headcount estimates vary (11–50 vs. <100).
