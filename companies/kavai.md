# KavAI (formerly SoterAI)

- **Site:** https://www.kavai.com/ (old: soterai.com)
- **Founders:** Sam Bigdeli, Tara Javidi, Meir Feder
- **Category:** Foundation model for industrial / physical-world sensing and prediction

## What they do in physical AI

Not a robot maker. KavAI builds an **adaptive AI foundation model for physical-world intelligence**: monitoring and predicting conditions in industrial settings. Stated target sectors: **robotics, manufacturing, energy**.

Read as: models over sensor/operational data from physical systems (predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, state forecasting) rather than manipulation or locomotion. Co-founders Tara Javidi and Meir Feder are information theory / ML academics, which fits a learning-from-sensor-streams approach.
