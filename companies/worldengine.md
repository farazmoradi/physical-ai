# WorldEngine

- **Site:** https://worldengine.ai/ (also listed at world-engine.ai)
- **HQ:** Santa Clara, CA
- **Founded:** 2025
- **Stage:** Seed; 10–50 people
- **Investors:** ZWC Partners, UpScaleX
- **Category:** Data + post-training infrastructure for physical AI

## What they do in physical AI

Tagline: **"Physical AGI."** Mission: **Physical AGI, starting with scaling physical experience by 10,000x.**

The thesis is that robots are bottlenecked on real-world experience data. WorldEngine runs a closed loop:

1. **Capture** real-world physical data
2. **Structure** it
3. **Train** / post-train models
4. **Evaluate**
5. **Deploy**, with real-world failures fed back into the next training cycle

Talk title: "World Engine: Towards the Era of Post-Training for Physical AI". They frame themselves as the post-training data engine for robot / embodied foundation models ("Physical AGI data solutions").

## Take

A "Scale AI for robotics" type data play, with an emphasis on post-training and a data flywheel driven by deployment failures.

## Caveats

- F4 Fund describes a "WorldEngine" open-source post-training framework for **autonomous driving** data scarcity. Could be an earlier focus or a different project; unconfirmed.
- PitchBook classifies it as business/productivity software.
- Robotics Connected cites a report of a **$100M annualised run rate**. No primary source found; treat as unverified.
