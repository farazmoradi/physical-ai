Five physical AI startups, side by side

All five help robots work in the real world, but each one works on a different part of the job. Below: which steps each company covers, where they sit on the map, and a plain-words summary of each.

Which steps of making a useful robot does each company work on?

Read left to right as the life of a robot: gather examples, practise in a simulated world, learn, get tested, go to work, then keep running. Dark cells are the company's main business; light cells are things it touches on.

Who do they sell to, and do they work in simulation or in the real world?

Positions are my judgment from public info, not measurements. Hover or tab to a dot for a one-line summary.

Left side, robot builders. Veeda, Kite and WorldEngine sell to companies that make robots. They supply the practice worlds, the training tools or the data.

Top versus bottom. Veeda and Kite bet on simulation (practise in a virtual world). WorldEngine bets on real-world data being what's missing.

Right side, factories. Shiraz and KavAI sell straight to industry. Shiraz brings the working robot itself; KavAI brings AI that watches existing machines.

Empty corner. Nobody here sells simulation tools straight to factories.
